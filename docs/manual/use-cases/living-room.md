## The case for Switchboard

- **One remote instead of five or six switches and remotes.** Lights, scenes, blinds, heating, TV and music are all pages of the same remote. Nobody hunts for the right one.
- **Anyone can use it.** Buttons say *Movie night*, *All off* and *Close blinds*, not *Scene 3*. Visitors and the babysitter don't need a tour.
- **No phone needed.** Nobody has to unlock a phone and find an app to dim the lights mid-film.
- **It lives in the room.** A remote belongs to its room and shows that room's lights and speakers first. Pick another room in Settings to take it elsewhere, with nothing to re-flash.
- **It's not another screen glowing at you.** E-ink has no glow, and a shade lights it in the dark.

Make a layout for the room with the pages you want, and name the buttons the way your household talks. See [Remote layouts](../server/remote-layouts.md) and [Screens](../remote/screens.md).
