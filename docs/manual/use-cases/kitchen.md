## The case for Switchboard

- **Everyone sees it, nobody has to open an app.** The things a household asks each other ("Is it going to rain?", "Did anyone lock up?", "Is the battery full?") are on the fridge.
- **Only what matters right now.** The *Now* list shows a sentence when something needs attention, such as a window open with the heating on, and nothing when all is well.
- **No cable to the fridge.** A viewport needs no power cable and runs for around 3 months on a charge.
- **No glow in the kitchen at night.** It reads like paper in daylight and is dark at night, unlike a tablet.
- **Answers that come from your house.** Energy, heating and security come straight from Home Assistant, with no template sensors to write.

## Setting it up

Start from the kitchen dashboard (**Start from or import → Kitchen dashboard**), then change its screens and sections in [Viewport layouts](../server/viewports.md); the sections are described in [Screens](../viewport/screens.md).
