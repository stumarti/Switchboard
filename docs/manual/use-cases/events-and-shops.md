## The case for Switchboard

- **Signs that change without reprinting.** Room changes, the next talk and *Doors open* come from a calendar, so the sign is right all day.
- **Put them anywhere.** No power needed at the easel or in the window.
- **Readable in sunlight.** E-ink looks like print in a bright window, where a screen washes out.
- **Your photos and live information together.** A board can show a photo from Immich with the weather, opening hours or today's offer beside it.
- **Change every board from one page.** Update the special or the room once and every sign follows.
