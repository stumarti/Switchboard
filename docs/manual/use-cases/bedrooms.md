# Bedrooms and kids' rooms

<p class="lead">In a bedroom you want the blinds down, the heating right and the lights off without getting up. In a child's room you want them to have their own lights and a nightlight, and nothing else to wander into.</p>

## The case for Switchboard

- **Replaces the switches by the door and the thermostat on the landing.** The lights, blinds and the room's heating are on the remote by the bed.
- **A child's remote shows only their room.** Give it a layout with their lights, their nightlight colours and their speaker. There's nothing on it that turns off the rest of the house.
- **Plain English, big buttons.** Children who can read *Bedtime* and *Nightlight* can use it. Younger ones learn where the buttons are.
- **Dark at night.** E-ink doesn't glow, so it doesn't light up the room or keep anyone awake. The shade lights it only when you need it.
- **A month on a charge.** It lives on the bedside table, not on a cable.

## Setting it up

Give each bedroom its own [remote layout](../server/remote-layouts.md). The [Climate](../remote/screens.md) page controls the room's heating zone.
